# Fitness Personas

SIMULATED — grounded in GA4 data across three sites (Garage Gym Reviews, BarBend, Fortune) and internal persona notes. These personas are synthesized ACROSS all three brands around shared behaviors and motivations — none are brand-specific. Hair loss content is treated as its own separate category and excluded here. Use only to simulate independent reactions — do not let these override real user research if it exists.

## The Home Gym Builder
**Sample voice:** "I'm not just buying a rack, I want to know it'll hold up under real use. Show me the specs, not just 'built for serious lifters.'"

- **Traffic share:** Reflected in GGR's Money Bucket (89.8K sessions) and Attack (30.9K sessions) content
- **Behavior:** High-intent equipment buyer, converts on Money Bucket/Attack-style pages. Researches a specific category deeply (rack, functional trainer, dumbbells) before buying rather than browsing broadly. Desktop + mobile balanced.
- **Motivations:** Build out serious home training capacity — wants durable, capable equipment, not a starter product.
- **Trusts:** Named brands with proven track records (Rep Fitness, Rogue, Titan, PowerBlock), detailed specs.
- **Distrusts:** Vague "great for everyone" equipment claims without real specs.
- **Demographics:** General — no reliable segment-specific demographic data available across GGR/Fortune; BarBend skews male 25-34 but not extended here since it's site-specific, not confirmed category-wide.
- **Data sources:** GGR Money Bucket pages ($1.35 RPV, 89.8K sessions), Attack pages ($1.84 RPV, highest non-coupon RPV).

## The Recovery & Wellness Investor
**Sample voice:** "I've read enough about the actual recovery science to know what I want. Don't just tell me it's trendy — tell me it works."

- **Traffic share:** Thinner than Home Gym Builder but notably higher RPV ($4-5 vs $0.76-1.84)
- **Behavior:** Big-ticket buyer investing in cold plunges, saunas, recovery tools. Desktop research, often finishes purchase on mobile. Same decisive, high-RPV behavior as Home Gym Builder, but motivated by recovery/longevity rather than training capacity.
- **Motivations:** Invest in long-term physical resilience and recovery capacity, not just performance in the moment.
- **Trusts:** Wellness/biohacking credibility, real specs on recovery claims (temperature ranges, materials, etc).
- **Distrusts:** Recovery products framed as trendy gimmicks without real substance behind the claims.
- **Demographics:** General — no segment-specific demographic data available.
- **Data sources:** Cross-site "Recovery Enthusiast" persona ($4-5 RPV); brands Plunge, Sun Home Saunas, Redwood Outdoors identified across BarBend/GGR data.

## The Comparison Researcher
**Sample voice:** "I've got five tabs open comparing these. I'm not ready to buy yet — I just want to understand what's actually different between them."

- **Traffic share:** Largest content-type by sessions on GGR (96.6K) and significant on BarBend (30.1K)
- **Behavior:** Broad roundup browser, visits many pages, converts rarely on any single visit. Present in both equipment (GGR roundups) and supplements (BarBend roundups) alike — this is a decision-stage behavior, not a product-category identity.
- **Motivations:** Feel confident in a decision before committing money — still in the "still deciding" phase, not ready to act yet.
- **Trusts:** Comprehensive, honest comparisons that clearly explain tradeoffs.
- **Distrusts:** Roundups that feel like they're steering toward one predetermined winner rather than giving an honest comparison.
- **Demographics:** General — no segment-specific demographic data available.
- **Data sources:** GGR Roundup content (96,617 sessions, $77,482 revenue); BarBend Roundup content (30,136 sessions, $11,353 revenue).

## The Performance Supplement Optimizer
**Sample voice:** "What's actually in this? I don't want a proprietary blend that hides the dosages — and honestly, at this point I'm also curious about longevity stuff, not just my next lift."

- **Traffic share:** BarBend's dominant supplement category; NMN a small but present secondary interest (18K views, Fortune)
- **Behavior:** Recurring, lower-ticket purchases (protein, creatine, pre-workout) tied to athletic/muscle-building performance. Mobile-first, quick visits, comparison-shops via roundups before buying. Secondary motivation: longevity/NMN supplements as a proactive healthspan interest alongside performance goals.
- **Motivations:** Optimize physical performance and, increasingly, long-term healthspan — wants transparency in what they're actually putting in their body.
- **Trusts:** Ingredient transparency, third-party testing, credible dosage information.
- **Distrusts:** Proprietary blends that hide dosages. Vague longevity claims without real backing.
- **Demographics:** General — no segment-specific demographic data reliably available across sites.
- **Data sources:** BarBend Supplement Optimizer persona ($0.38 RPV, mobile-first); brands Transparent Labs, Jacked Factory, KOS Naturals, Nutricost. Longevity/NMN folded in from Fortune's NMN supplement content (18K views) — thinner data, treat as secondary/tentative.

## The Deal Hunter
**Sample voice:** "I already know I want this brand — I'm just looking for the code before I check out."

- **Traffic share:** Small session volume but highest RPV of any content type across both sites
- **Behavior:** Already brand-aware, searches specifically for a coupon/discount code, converts fast once found. Mobile-heavy. Shows up on both GGR ($6.00 RPV) and BarBend ($3.41 RPV) — a behavior pattern, not a brand loyalty.
- **Motivations:** Get a product they've already decided on, but at the best possible price — the coupon is the last step, not the research phase.
- **Trusts:** Visible, legitimate discount codes that actually work at checkout.
- **Distrusts:** Expired or fake-feeling coupon pages that don't actually deliver a working discount.
- **Demographics:** General — no segment-specific demographic data available.
- **Data sources:** GGR Core Coupon (4,363 sessions, $26,163 revenue, $6.00 RPV); BarBend Core Coupon (365 sessions, $1,245 revenue, $3.41 RPV); brands NordicTrack, Higher Dose, BowFlex.

## The Fitness Content Consumer / Community Member
**Sample voice:** "I'm here almost every week reading about training and technique. I'm not really shopping right now, I just like this stuff."

- **Traffic share:** Reflected in GGR's high engagement time and BarBend's large direct-traffic base; low/no direct revenue attribution
- **Behavior:** Reads workout guides and technique content, rarely converts, but represents the loyal, returning, high-engagement audience. Reflected in GGR's 1m17s average engagement time and BarBend's direct-traffic dominance (216K direct new users). Here for information and community, not a purchase.
- **Motivations:** Stay informed and engaged with the fitness space — not driven by an immediate purchase need.
- **Trusts:** Genuine expertise, no hard sell, content written by people who clearly know the subject.
- **Distrusts:** Content that's obviously just a wrapper for an affiliate link rather than genuine expertise.
- **Demographics:** General — no segment-specific demographic data reliably available.
- **Data sources:** GGR engagement overview (1m17s avg engagement); BarBend acquisition (Direct 216K new users, largest single channel).

## The Fast-Decision Paid Searcher
**Sample voice:** "I searched for exactly this. If this page doesn't confirm it's the thing I was looking for in the first few seconds, I'm gone."

- **Traffic share:** Dominant pattern on Fortune (paid search >> all other channels combined); smaller presence on GGR
- **Behavior:** Arrives via a paid ad already knowing exactly what they searched (a specific supplement or product category), engages briefly (~26s), and either converts or bounces fast. Dominant pattern on Fortune, present in smaller form on GGR's paid channel.
- **Motivations:** Get a fast answer to a specific, already-formed need — not here to browse or be educated, here to confirm and act.
- **Trusts:** The page immediately confirming it's the exact thing they searched for.
- **Distrusts:** Any friction or mismatch between the ad promise and the landing page.
- **Demographics:** General — Fortune has no demographic data available (gender/age/interests all show "No data available"); not extending BarBend's demographic skew here since it's a different site/channel mix.
- **Data sources:** Fortune acquisition data (Paid Search dominant new-user channel, ~171K mobile-heavy sessions, 26s avg engagement); GGR paid channel ($13.6K revenue via targeted ads) as a smaller parallel.
